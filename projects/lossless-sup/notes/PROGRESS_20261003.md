## RAW-View merges too: the FPLM block follows the read cap (same day)

The launcher no longer reads FPLM at a fixed 0xF000. menu_page.c reads the
16-byte VBIN header, works out used = 16 + 8*count + body, and takes the data
from max(0xF000, used rounded up to 4 KiB) -- the read cap fpSup-Merge gives
that card (capFor), at most 0x1F000. The skip is read in pieces no bigger
than the room. struct fpl_menu gains file_at. A card whose VBIN fits 0xF000
is laid out exactly as before (release AutoRun and FPLM unchanged; launcher
24,000 -> 24,240 B, 4d99d496…). build_card.py uses the same rule
(menu_at_for), building once at 0x1F000 to learn the size and again at the
cap it needs; -DFPL_MENU_AT / BIG_READ are gone.

Composer: the tail goes at vshl.cap. Nothing is refused any more: 48 of 214
cases put it past 0xF000 (lossless + raw-view + gyro/OG). test_compose also
checks the fast stage2's rewritten magic now that such cards exist.
Offline: page's shell+lossless+og3k+raw-view+FS2 vs build_card --og og3k
--raw-view: used 0x1083C both, FPLM at 0x11000 both, AutoRun identical, same
sections, entries worker -> lossless -> OG restore -> raw-view in both; only
the trampoline's offsets differ.
Tests: test_menu_page 31 OK (6 new mutations caught), card emulation 15 OK,
codec_job / frame_hold / play_decode OK, test_compose and test_catalog_ui PASS.
fpsup-lossless-v0.1.2test (not yet published) was rebuilt with this launcher.
NOT on the camera: the new menu read; a raw-view merge card at all.
