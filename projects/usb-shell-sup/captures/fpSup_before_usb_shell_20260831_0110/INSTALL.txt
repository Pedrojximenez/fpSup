fpGyroSup v1 -- SIGMA fp, firmware Ver.5.02 only

Copy everything beside this file -- AutoRun.txt, VSHL.BIN, PGEN.BIN and the
GYRO folder -- into the ROOT of an SD card. Put the card in the camera and
power on; a progress bar runs to "fpSup!", about 18 seconds. Then shoot.

Each CinemaDNG clip then leaves three files on the card:

    \GYRO\A001_017.GYR                 raw gyro log
    \CINEMA\A001_017\A001_017.gcsv     gyro + accelerometer, for Gyroflow
    \CINEMA\A001_017\A001_017.json     lens profile, for Gyroflow

MOV is not covered yet. A MOV take still gets its .GYR, so the gyro data is
captured, but no .gcsv and no .json -- both are named from the CinemaDNG clip
folder that a MOV does not have. Recording is unaffected.

Nothing is flashed. This is a RAM injection: take the battery out and the
camera is exactly as it was. Remove the three files from the card to stop it.

Full notes: https://github.com/ijigen/fpSup/tree/main/gyro/release

不影響錄影。

就停用。

僅適用韌體 Ver.5.02。
