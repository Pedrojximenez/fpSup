STOPFX

Helps the camera clear pending frames after an abnormal recording stop.

幫助相機在異常停錄後清理暫存影格。

By 作者：Ruari · Test build測試版 · 2026-10-12

Install安裝

Select STOPFX in fpSup-Merge and follow its installation steps. SIGMA fp firmware Ver.5.02 only.

在 fpSup-Merge 勾選 STOPFX，依頁面說明安裝。只支援 SIGMA fp 韌體 Ver.5.02。

Independent of Lossless可獨立使用

STOPFX works as soon as the camera starts; no extra setting is needed. It can run alone or alongside Lossless. Select each tool separately in Merge.

勾選後，開機即生效，不用另外設定。可單獨使用，也能與 Lossless 同卡；在 Merge 分別勾選即可。

What it changes修正效果

If recording stops unexpectedly, STOPFX helps clear frames still waiting to be saved. It does not recover lost frames or make storage faster.

錄影意外停止時，幫助相機清理仍在等待保存的影格。它不會補回丟失影格，也不會提高卡片速度。

Camera checks上機紀錄

On 2026-10-12, STOPFX and Lossless were used together through ten consecutive overload stops at 3K, 12-bit, 100 fps without restarting the camera. A subsequent FHD 59.94 fps recording completed.

2026-10-12 與 Lossless 同卡，在 3K、12-bit、100 fps 連續觸發 10 次過載停錄，中間沒有重開機，之後仍完成 FHD 59.94 fps 錄影。

Overload can still lose frames, and STOPFX does not fix separate Lossless recording stalls. SSD recording and repeated restarts have not been checked in these trials.

過載仍可能丟格，STOPFX 也不能修正 Lossless 本身的錄影卡住問題。這次尚未檢查 SSD 錄影及反覆重開機。
