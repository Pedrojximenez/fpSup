fpSup-Desqueeze

Restore anamorphic proportions in the preview at 1.33x, 1.6x or 2.0x. The full frame is letterboxed; recorded CinemaDNG files remain unchanged.

以 1.33x、1.6x 或 2.0x 還原變形鏡頭的預覽比例。完整畫面上下留黑邊，錄下的 CinemaDNG 檔案不變。

By 作者：Jose Hurtado · v0.1.4test · test build測試版

Install安裝

Select Desqueeze in fpSup-Merge and follow its installation steps. SIGMA fp firmware Ver.5.02 only.

在 fpSup-Merge 勾選 Desqueeze，依頁面說明安裝。只支援 SIGMA fp 韌體 Ver.5.02。

Choose the factor選擇倍率

MENU → SHOOT 2 → Desqueeze · Off / 1.33x / 1.6x / 2.0x

Available in CINE and STILL. Frame guides and the magnifier’s white frame follow the preview proportions. Amber lines mark the frame edges and a factor label appears under the battery indicator during live view and playing video.

CINE 與 STILL 都可使用。構圖線與放大檢視的白框跟著預覽比例縮放。即時檢視及播放中的影片會以琥珀色線標示畫面邊緣，電池圖示下方顯示倍率。

Remembered on the card設定保存在卡上

The factor is saved in FPSUP.INI on the card. This product does not change the camera’s saved settings.

倍率保存在卡上的 FPSUP.INI；這項功能不改寫相機的保存設定。

Using both tools與觸控放大搭配

You can also select Tap to Zoom. The enlarged picture fills the LCD while keeping the desqueeze proportions.

可同時勾選 Tap to Zoom；放大後的畫面填滿 LCD，並保持還原比例。

Known limits已知限制

  • EVF and HDMI have not yet been checked by the author.
  • The magnifier frame immediately after a cold boot is still unverified. Leaving the menu rebuilds the live view and refreshes the frame.
  • The native CINE magnifier’s 4x/8x switch can sometimes have no effect; the author has not traced that to Desqueeze.
  • 作者尚未驗證 EVF 與 HDMI。
  • 冷開機後、第一次進出選單之前，放大框是否已正確仍待驗證；離開選單會重建即時畫面並更新放大框。
  • CINE 的原廠放大檢視 4x/8x 切換有時沒有反應；作者尚未確認是否由 Desqueeze 引起。

Verification驗證紀錄

Jose reports camera tests on 2026-10-09 with Lossless, Gyro2, Res-Custom, RAW-View and both monitor tools. The fpSup integration checks the original file hashes, Merge downloads and loading under the current loader in offline emulation. See the original README for the author’s test details.

JOSE 回報在 2026-10-09 與 Lossless、Gyro2、Res-Custom、RAW-View 及這兩項監看工具同卡實測。fpSup 整合核對原始檔案雜湊、Merge 下載內容，並以離線模擬檢查目前載入器的載入行為。作者的實測細節見原始 README。
